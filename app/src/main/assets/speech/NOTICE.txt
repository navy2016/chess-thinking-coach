Edge speech protocol integration

EdgeGec.kt is a Kotlin port of drm.py from edge-tts 7.3.1, commit 360fa8e1de354e1dda74c09bd695887d6a44df74.

Modifications: Kotlin/JVM implementation, standard round loop instead of unrolled Python rounds, no clock correction or retry after service denial.
Original source: https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts/tree/360fa8e1de354e1dda74c09bd695887d6a44df74
Modified source and build instructions: https://github.com/navy2016/chess-thinking-coach
Corresponding Kotlin source is included with this release's source archive.
